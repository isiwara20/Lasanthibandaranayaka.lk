Styling Studio

The Main Styling Lounge

Step into our spacious main styling studio featuring custom halo-lit arched vanity mirrors, plush ergonomic treatment chairs, and polished marble flooring. Here, our expert stylists perform precision haircuts, hair spa therapies, color transformations, and personalized consultations.

  • Individual vanity stations with halo backlighting
  • Professional Dyson & Italian hair styling tools
  • Relaxing background music & climate control

Private VIP Bridal Dressing Suite

Your wedding morning deserves unhurried peace and privacy. Our dedicated VIP Bridal Suite offers an exclusive haven where brides, grooms, and bridal retinues can dress, relax, and savor refreshments in royal privacy with their own stylist team.

  • 360-degree full-length bridal mirror setup
